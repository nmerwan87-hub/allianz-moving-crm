# Bivro — Business Model

---

## 1. Vision

**To become the global operating system for the moving and relocation industry.**

Just as Salesforce became the system of record for customer relationships and Stripe became the infrastructure for internet commerce, Bivro will become the platform that every moving company, relocation manager, and logistics operator in the world runs their business on — from the first customer inquiry to the final payment, powered end-to-end by artificial intelligence.

Bivro does not build software for moving companies. Bivro builds the intelligence layer that transforms a fragmented, paper-driven, operationally chaotic industry into a modern, predictive, and self-optimizing business.

---

## 2. Mission

**To give every moving business in the world the operational power of a Fortune 500 company.**

The moving industry is one of the largest service industries on earth — and one of the least technologically enabled. Millions of people relocate every year. Tens of thousands of companies serve them. Most of those companies run on spreadsheets, phone calls, and tribal knowledge. Drivers miss windows. Quotes are wrong. Customers are left waiting. Revenue leaks at every step.

Bivro's mission is to eliminate that gap. To give a three-truck operation in Lyon the same operational intelligence as a national relocation company in New York — through AI that quotes, schedules, dispatches, communicates, invoices, and learns, automatically.

We measure success not by revenue, but by moves completed on time, customers who would recommend their mover, and companies that grew because of Bivro.

---

## 3. Core Values

**1. Intelligence over process.**
We do not digitize bad workflows. We replace them with AI that thinks ahead — predicting demand, catching errors before they happen, and surfacing the decision before the operator knew they had one.

**2. The operator is the hero.**
Moving company owners work 60-hour weeks. They are not looking for software — they are looking for relief. Every feature we build must save time, reduce anxiety, or make money for the operator. If it doesn't do one of those three things, it doesn't ship.

**3. Radical simplicity.**
Enterprise power, consumer simplicity. Bivro should feel like WhatsApp, not SAP. A dispatcher with no technical background should be productive in their first hour. Complexity is hidden, not presented.

**4. Data as a shared asset.**
Every company that uses Bivro makes Bivro smarter. Every quote, every job, every customer interaction trains models that benefit all operators. This is not extraction — it is compounding value returned to every participant in the network.

**5. Trust is infrastructure.**
Moving companies handle people's lives — their furniture, their memories, their homes. We treat customer data with the same seriousness as a bank treats financial data. Privacy, security, and compliance are non-negotiable foundations, not features.

**6. Global by default.**
We build for Casablanca and Chicago, for São Paulo and Singapore. Language, currency, regulation, and workflow are configuration — not limitations. We design every feature assuming it will need to work in 50 countries.

## 4. Target Customers

Bivro serves the full spectrum of the global moving and relocation industry, organized into four segments:

### Segment A — Independent Operators
Small moving companies: 1–10 trucks, 2–20 employees, owner-operated. The most underserved segment. Currently using WhatsApp groups, paper forms, and phone-based dispatch. Extremely price-sensitive. Volume: hundreds of thousands globally. Entry point for the Bivro network.

### Segment B — Regional Movers
Mid-market moving companies: 10–100 trucks, multiple crews, dedicated office staff. Have attempted software before (spreadsheets, legacy CRM, custom tools) but nothing built for them. Budget-conscious but willing to pay for measurable ROI. The core SaaS revenue engine.

### Segment C — National & Enterprise Movers
Large moving companies and van lines: 100+ trucks, multiple branches, franchise networks. Need multi-location management, fleet tracking, workforce scheduling, revenue reporting. Require enterprise security, SLA commitments, and dedicated support. High ACV, long sales cycles.

### Segment D — Corporate Relocation
HR departments, third-party relocation management companies (RMCs), and corporate mobility managers who coordinate employee relocations at scale. Need vendor management, compliance documentation, cost reporting, and integration with HRMS platforms. Adjacent market with significant budget and global reach.

## 7. Pricing Strategy

Bivro uses a **value-based, usage-anchored pricing model** with a freemium entry tier to maximize top-of-funnel, and platform fees to monetize at scale. Pricing is designed to grow with the customer — as they do more jobs, Bivro earns more.

### Tier Structure

---

#### Free — "Explorer"
**$0 / 14-day trial, then account paused**

For solo operators testing the platform. Limited to 5 jobs and 10 quotes during the trial. Includes: basic AI quoting (manual mode only), job calendar, customer records, invoice generation. No credit card required. Designed for acquisition, not retention. Upsell trigger: hitting the job/quote ceiling or the 14-day trial expiry.

> **Naming note:** The internal DB value is `'free'`. The product UI label is "Free" or "Explorer." Do not use "Starter" for this tier — "Starter" is the paid entry tier defined below.

---

#### Starter
**$49 / month** (billed annually: $39/month)

For sole operators and micro-companies (1–3 movers) running a real business. Up to 2 office users, 30 jobs/month, 50,000 AI tokens/month, 5 GB storage. Includes: full AI quoting engine, crew dispatch, customer communication, PDF documents (quotes, contracts, invoices), email automation (12 built-in rules), basic reporting. Single location. No credit card-based payments (Stripe add-on in Business). Upsell trigger: hitting the job ceiling or needing multiple locations or advanced analytics.

> Internal DB value: `'starter'`. Product name: "Starter."

---

#### Growth — "Pro"
**$199 / month** (billed annually: $159/month)

For independent operators running a real business. Unlimited jobs. Includes: full AI quoting engine, crew dispatch, customer communication automation, PDF documents (quotes, contracts, invoices), email sequences, basic reporting, mobile app for crews. Up to 5 users. 1 location.

---

#### Scale — "Business"
**$499 / month** (billed annually: $399/month)

For regional companies. Everything in Pro plus: multi-location support, fleet management, advanced analytics, API access, Stripe payment processing, QuickBooks integration, crew GPS tracking, up to 25 users. Priority support.

---

#### Enterprise
**Custom pricing** (typically $2,000–$20,000/month)

For national movers, van lines, and corporate relocation. Everything in Business plus: dedicated infrastructure, SLA guarantees, custom integrations (HRMS, ERP), white-labeling, dedicated customer success manager, SSO, advanced compliance reporting, unlimited users and locations.

---

#### AI Credits (Add-on)
Each tier includes a monthly AI credit allocation. Additional credits can be purchased. Credits power: AI quoting on complex jobs, AI damage assessment from photos, predictive demand forecasting, AI-generated customer communications, automated dispute resolution.

**Credit pricing:** $0.10 per credit, sold in bundles of 500/1,000/5,000.

## 8. Revenue Streams

Bivro is built for **multiple compounding revenue streams** — not just subscription fees. This is the structural difference between a CRM vendor and a platform company.

### Stream 1 — SaaS Subscriptions
Recurring monthly and annual subscription revenue across all tiers. This is the foundation: predictable, compounding, low churn if the product delivers value. Target gross margin: 80%+.

### Stream 2 — Payment Processing (Take Rate)
Bivro processes payments between moving companies and their customers via Stripe Connect. Bivro earns a take rate on every transaction processed through the platform. At scale, this becomes as large as the subscription revenue. Target: 0.5–1.5% per transaction. This is the Stripe model applied to a vertical.

### Stream 3 — AI Credits
Usage-based revenue on top of subscriptions. As AI capabilities expand (damage photo assessment, complex inventory recognition, automated dispute mediation), usage pricing captures value proportional to what the AI actually does. This is the AWS model applied to intelligence.

### Stream 4 — Marketplace / Lead Generation
Bivro connects customers looking for moving services with verified Bivro-powered operators. A lead marketplace where operators bid on or receive matched jobs. Revenue model: per-qualified-lead fee ($15–$50) or a success fee on booked jobs (3–5% of job value). This creates a network effect: more operators attract more customers attract more operators.

### Stream 5 — Embedded Insurance
Partnership with insurance carriers to offer on-demand moving insurance to customers at point of booking. Bivro earns a referral/distribution fee per policy. This aligns with the transaction: every move carries risk, every customer wants protection, most movers don't offer it well today.

### Stream 6 — Data Intelligence (Enterprise)
Anonymized, aggregated market intelligence sold to enterprise customers and industry stakeholders: average move pricing by market, demand seasonality, crew availability benchmarks, customer churn patterns. Not personal data — structural market intelligence. Think: Bloomberg for moving.

### Stream 7 — Partner Ecosystem (Future)
Third-party developers building on Bivro's API and platform. Revenue share on apps sold through the Bivro marketplace. This is the Salesforce AppExchange model — it extends Bivro's surface area without Bivro having to build everything.

## 9. Competitive Advantages

### Advantage 1 — AI-Native Architecture
Bivro is designed from the ground up with AI at the center, not added on top. The quoting engine, scheduling engine, and communication engine are AI-first. Competitors retrofitting AI onto legacy systems cannot match this without a full rebuild. This is a 3–5 year structural lead.

### Advantage 2 — Vertical Depth
Generic CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot) don't understand a cubic foot, a long-carry charge, or a COI request. Generic logistics platforms don't understand residential quoting or crew dispatch. Bivro understands the moving industry at an expert level — the language, the workflows, the edge cases, the regulations. This depth cannot be replicated by a horizontal player without years of domain investment.

### Advantage 3 — Data Compounding
Every job run on Bivro generates training data: what was quoted, what was actually charged, how long the job took, customer satisfaction. Over time, Bivro's AI models become more accurate than any individual company's internal knowledge. The platform gets smarter as it grows — creating a flywheel that competitors cannot replicate without the same data volume.

### Advantage 4 — Full-Stack Coverage
Bivro covers the entire job lifecycle: lead → quote → booking → contract → crew dispatch → tracking → completion → damage documentation → invoice → payment → review collection → retention. No competitor covers this full arc. Operators using Bivro replace 4–7 tools, not 1. This creates switching costs that grow over time.

### Advantage 5 — Network Effects
The marketplace creates a two-sided network: operators on one side, customers on the other. As the operator network grows, the marketplace becomes more valuable to customers. As the customer base grows, operators generate more revenue and become more dependent on Bivro. This is a flywheel that accelerates independently of product feature investment.

### Advantage 6 — Global Infrastructure
Built multi-currency, multi-language, and multi-regulatory from day one. When a competitor decides to expand internationally, they face years of re-engineering. Bivro treats global as the default, not a future version.

Bivro becomes the horizontal infrastructure for the on-site service economy.

**The 10-year ambition:** Bivro is the Stripe of the physical service economy. Not a niche vertical software vendor — a foundational platform that powers an entire category of human economic activity.

| Payback Period | 12 months | 8 months | 6 months |
| Churn (monthly) | 3% | 1.8% | 1.2% |

### Growth Model
Bivro grows through three compounding mechanisms:

**New Logo Acquisition:** New operators joining the platform through direct, partner, and marketplace channels.

**Expansion Revenue:** Existing operators upgrading tiers as their business grows, activating payments, purchasing AI credits. Net Revenue Retention above 100% means the existing customer base grows revenue without adding new customers.

**Marketplace Revenue:** Non-subscription revenue that scales with job volume, not seat count. As the operator network matures, marketplace and payment processing revenue accelerates faster than subscription revenue.

### Key SaaS Metrics
- **ARR:** Primary top-line metric
- **NRR (Net Revenue Retention):** Health of the existing base — target 120%+ at maturity
- **Magic Number:** Sales efficiency — target above 0.75
- **Gross Margin:** Target 80%+ at scale (infrastructure costs drop with volume)
- **Rule of 40:** Revenue growth % + EBITDA margin % > 40 (target 60+ at maturity)

## 13. Product Philosophy

**Bivro is a platform, not a product.**

A product solves a single problem. A platform becomes the foundation on which operators build and run their entire business. The distinction is strategic: product companies compete on features; platform companies win on ecosystem, data, and switching costs.

### The Five Product Principles

**1. AI-first, not AI-enhanced.**
Every workflow in Bivro starts with the question: "What would this look like if AI did it?" not "How do we add AI to this?" The quote is not generated by a form — the AI reads the customer description and produces the quote. The schedule is not built by dragging blocks — the AI proposes the optimal schedule. The operator confirms or adjusts; the AI learns.

**2. Operators should never have to ask twice.**
If an operator had to answer a question — to a customer, to a crew member, to an accountant — Bivro should have been able to answer it automatically. Every manual touchpoint is a product failure. We relentlessly identify and eliminate the moments where operators are interrupted to provide information the system should already know.

**3. Mobile-first for crews, desktop-complete for offices.**
Crew members live on their phones. Dispatchers live on a dashboard. Accountants live in reports. Bivro serves all three surfaces with equal quality. The mobile app is not a simplified version of the desktop — it is a different, optimized experience for a different context.

**4. The platform earns trust by staying invisible.**
The best version of Bivro is one where operators say "I don't even think about the quote anymore — Bivro handles it." The software that gets noticed is the software that goes wrong. Excellence in execution means Bivro works so reliably, so accurately, and so quietly that operators simply focus on serving their customers.

**5. AI assists. Humans decide.**
Every AI recommendation in Bivro is a proposal, never a mandate. Every AI-generated output — a quote, a crew suggestion, an email draft — is editable, overridable, and rejectable by the operator. The AI earns trust by being right most of the time; the operator retains authority because it is their business, their customers, and their reputation. The system must be designed so that no operator ever feels locked into an AI decision they disagree with.
